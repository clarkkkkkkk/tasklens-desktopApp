# Code of conduct

TaskLens welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful, constructive and patient. Give technical feedback about work, credit contributors, preserve shared changes and respect privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted attention, deliberate exposure of private information and retaliation are unacceptable. This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful material and warn, temporarily restrict or remove participants according to severity and repeated behavior. They should explain decisions privately where possible, protect reporters and handle conflicts of interest fairly. Affected participants may request review by another available maintainer.

For confidential reporting, follow the contact procedure in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not disclose personal details or allegations in a public issue. The owner must establish a private reporting channel before inviting broader community participation.

Adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This concise project policy does not claim that a private reporting service is already enabled.
